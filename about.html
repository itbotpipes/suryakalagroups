Who We Are

Rooted in Tradition, Driven by Precision

Founded in Thayilpatti, Tamil Nadu — the hub of India's match manufacturing industry — Sri Suryakala Match Industries has grown into a trusted leader across domestic and international markets.

We prioritize customer satisfaction at every step. From custom graphic design and box manufacturing to moisture-proof wrapping and container dispatch, our team ensures a seamless experience.

Our matchboxes are renowned for single-strike ignition, sturdy splints, non-drooping flames, and longer burning times, meeting international standard specifications.
